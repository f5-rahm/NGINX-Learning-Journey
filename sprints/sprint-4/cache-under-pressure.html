Reading X-Cache-Status

StatusPath that produced itOrigin contacted for this request?
HITFresh entry, or filled while waiting on the lockNo
UPDATINGExpired entry that another request is refreshing; updating allowedNo
STALEBackground update served the old copy, or the origin failed with a listed conditionNo wait for the client. A background subrequest may be fetching.
MISSNo entry: no lock, the lock holder, or a waiter that timed outYes
EXPIREDExpired entry refreshed in the foreground, with a new 200 or a passed-through errorYes
REVALIDATEDConditional refresh answered 304Yes, with no body
BYPASSproxy_cache_bypass firedYes

What to watch in monitoring

  • A burst of MISS or EXPIRED for one key right after expiry or a deploy is the herd. The lock fixes the MISS burst and updating fixes the EXPIRED burst.
  • With background update on, STALE also appears in normal operation, on the first request after every expiry. An outage looks like a sustained STALE rate while $upstream_status shows errors.
  • A slowest response near 5 seconds for a key that should collapse means waiters are hitting proxy_cache_lock_timeout. That's when the origin is slower than the lock.
